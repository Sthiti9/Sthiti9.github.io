About me

Hi! I'm a final-year Dual Degree student in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay, with a minor from the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science. I work with Prof. Laxmeesha Somappa on my Master's thesis, designing quantized autoencoders that compress neural (iEEG) signals for low-power, on-chip execution.

I like making intelligent systems small enough to live on a microcontroller. At ETH Zurich I fit a person detector onto a GAP9 inside a 360° camera ball. At TU Braunschweig I wrote low-power autonomous agents for nanosatellites. At Google I worked on kernel threading and on model evaluation. I want to bring this background to biomedical devices that are efficient, wearable and implantable.

Biomedical devices Embedded systems TinyML & model compression Neural signal processing Low-power hardware
