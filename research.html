Autoencoders for Neural Signal Compression

Master's Thesis · IIT Bombay · Advisor: Prof. Laxmeesha Somappa
  • Reduced bandwidth by more than 75% at under 10% error by benchmarking DCT, WHT and downsampling on seizure and non-seizure iEEG recordings from the SWEC-ETHZ database (MATLAB)
  • Designed a sparse autoencoder that improves on baseline Walsh-Hadamard Transform compression, then scaled the single-layer design into a two-stage autoencoder that handles channel and temporal mixing separately
  • Used quantization-aware training (QAT) so the model can run on low-power, low-compute on-chip hardware
